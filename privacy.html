Privacy Policy

OneClickQR is a free QR code generator that does not require an account. This policy explains what happens to information when you use www.oneclickqr.com, including its Google Analytics and Google AdSense integrations.

QR content and saved presets

QR text, links, Wi-Fi details, contact information, and uploaded logos are processed in your browser to create the preview and downloads. The generator does not send those form entries or images to a OneClickQR server, and its code does not explicitly send them to Google Analytics. If you choose to save a preset, its form entries, design settings, and any logo image are stored in your browser's local storage. You can delete a preset in the generator or clear this site's data in your browser.

When someone scans a QR code, their device may connect to the destination or use the information encoded in it. The destination's own privacy practices then apply.

Website visits and Google Analytics

Your browser sends ordinary request information, such as your IP address, browser details, and requested page, to the site's hosting provider to load the website. The homepage also uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits and site performance. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and collect page views, approximate location, and browser or device information. We use its reports to understand how the site is used and improve it. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services and read Google's Privacy Policy.

Google AdSense and advertising

The homepage includes Google AdSense code, so your browser may contact Google when it loads. When ads are served, third-party vendors, including Google, may place and read cookies or use web beacons, IP addresses, and other identifiers to provide and measure ads. Google and its advertising partners may use advertising cookies to show ads based on your visits to this site or other websites. Depending on the site's AdSense settings, other ad technology vendors or networks may also participate; Google publishes information about ad technology partners. See Google's explanation of data use on partner sites for more information.

Cookies and your choices

Google Analytics may set first-party cookies, and Google or other advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies for ads. Saved QR presets use browser local storage rather than cookies. You can manage or delete cookies and site data in your browser. You can manage Google's personalized ads through My Ad Center, opt out of some other vendors' personalized advertising at YourAdChoices, and use the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. These choices may not stop all ads or all data needed to deliver the site.
